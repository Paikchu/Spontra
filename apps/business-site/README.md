# Public business map

Independent Vite/React frontend and Cloudflare Worker; existing Spontra routes remain intact.

- The quarter is picked on the time axis under the chart (every report filing of the last two years, plus any flow quarter the filing list does not cover); the header only names the quarter on stage. Independently audited statements from existing SEC archives supplement the latest complete pair; choosing a report updates the metrics, original business classification, Sankey and trend selection together and opens that report in the lens. Historical comparison still requires matching disclosure definitions. No filing collection or AI work runs on page reads.
- Public entry: `/companies/ORCL`. `/` is the home page (Business View): the inline ticker search centred in the window (the search dialog's panel resting on the page; its list opens once the user types, ⌘K / `/` focus it) with recently opened companies beneath, and 要点 along the bottom as marquee rows of the stage's finding chips, each led by its company's logo instead of the severity mark. Companies take turns along the rows; a new row starts every twelve findings, up to three, alternating direction. Hover or keyboard focus holds a row; reduced motion turns rows into plain horizontal scrollers. A chip opens `/companies/:ticker?finding=<id>`. The chips cover the pipeline's findings companies (`SEC_AI_TICKERS`) plus recently opened ones, at most twelve companies, and each company is verified in the browser exactly as its page does (`company-data.ts`), so home never shows a finding the stage withholds. The rail's home button returns here. No portfolio or other tabs.
- Two-pane layout, no pan/zoom canvas: the business list (revenue tree, share, QoQ, sourced business notes) on the left; the full revenue → profit Sankey fitted to the right pane. Selecting a business traces its own slice through each parent band into total revenue, with a flowing animation and its share of revenue. Costs and profit are never allocated to a business. Its explanation opens in a scrolling card beside the Sankey (the stage keeps its height), and the canvas zooms to that business, its sub-businesses and its path into revenue; 适应画布 shows the whole statement again. A business can be picked from the list, its Sankey node or its own band; the list row is highlighted and scrolled into view (only the list scrolls, never the page) and the trend panel follows. Selection is kept in `?business=`.
- `FlowChart` renders from the shared graph (`financialGraph`) and layout (`layoutInfographic`, with one-line label geometry and a type scale solved per pane so labels stay legible). Signed, financial and insurance statements fall back to the shared `FinancialSankey` bridge. Business hues are a validated four-slot palette assigned per business, stable across quarters; further top-level businesses use a neutral tone.
- Below the Sankey, an eight-quarter revenue trend from the pipeline's `history` field: stacked by business for 全部业务, morphing into the selected business (its children stacked) with staggered height transitions. Quarters disclosed under another business presentation show company revenue or an explicit gap; fourth quarters derived as full year minus nine months are labelled.
- The bars carry a growth line on a right axis: 同比 (default when at least two points exist) or 环比 growth per quarter. Both axes share the four gridlines, 0% always on one of them, and the right labels and legend wear the line colour. Pointing at a dot or segment lights the line and right axis and dims the bars; pointing at a bar does the reverse. With the line drawn, only the current and hovered quarters carry value labels; the tooltip leads with the rate on the line and with the business split on a bar. A quarter without a comparable base is a gap, not zero.
- A metric picker beside the trend heading switches the bars from revenue by business (default) to one company-level SEC XBRL series from the `/fundamentals` response the page already loads (the former 财务指标 tab: profit, EPS, margins, cash flow, capex, cash, RPO), grouped by statement and listed only when at least two quarters are reported (`metric-model.ts`, `MetricTrend.tsx`). Bars stand on a zero line so losses and negative free cash flow hang below it; amounts carry a 同比 / 环比 growth line (a base at or below zero is a gap), and rates state their change in percentage points without a second line. Capex reads as a magnitude. Missing quarters stay gaps. A bar opens its quarter in the flow chart. The choice is kept in `?metric=`; the 增长来源 bridge and business split stay with revenue.
- Every Sankey node label carries its change against the prior quarter, using the same comparability rules as the list and tooltip; incomparable nodes show no change. Revenue and profit changes are colored by direction; costs stay neutral.
- Guidance on the chart itself (`guidance-marks.ts`): a bracket beside a bar is the last dollar range management guided for this quarter on that line, at the bar's scale (growth guidance uses the pipeline's derived amount and draws dashed), coloured by where the bar landed: 高于 (up), 落在 (neutral), 低于 (loss); its tooltip states the range, this quarter's figure, the verdict, the issue date, the verbatim quote and the source. A pill at the bar's middle is what the latest report guides for the next quarter on that line (下季 ↑ +30–34%, the glyph from the item's action), opening the source; its tooltip carries the sentence, the change against the previous guidance, the quote and the source. Lines the statement draws take marks: company revenue, a business whose filing name the guidance uses (`segmentMatches`), operating profit, and margins as pills only since a rate has no height. Constant-currency items, EPS, capex, cash flow and RPO stay in the trend panel. Marks need a USD quarter, rest while a hover, a pick, a finding or 对比上季 has the stage, and the legend names them only when drawn.
- Quarter over quarter on the chart itself (`business-flow-compare.ts`): each comparable bar wears a dashed frame of its prior-quarter amount at this quarter's scale, growing away from the label (top-labelled bars share their top, costs their bottom), so the gap between frame and bar is the change; a sign change or incomparable definition draws no frame. 对比上季 beside 适应画布 morphs the whole statement to the prior quarter laid out at this quarter's money-per-pixel and in this quarter's node order (bars, bands and labels glide; a node present in one quarter only fades with it), with a caption naming the prior period; finding marks, ghost frames and tooltips rest while the prior quarter shows. Offered only when the prior quarter shares currency, scale and a proportional statement; choosing another report returns to that report's own quarter. Reduced motion jumps instead of gliding.
- The trend panel's 增长来源 view is a revenue bridge for the selected quarter: the year-ago (同比, default when collected) or prior (环比) level, one floating step per business sorted by its change, then the current level, with each business's percentage-point contribution in the tooltip. The two ends are total bars standing on an axis cut above zero (marked with a break), with the floor set well below the lower total so the ends stay comparable while small business changes remain visible. A selected business is split by its children. When the two quarters are not stacked from the same businesses, the total change is one neutral step with the reason stated; a missing base quarter shows no bridge.
- The rail is a floating rounded card inset from the page edges. Its pinned top holds the company mark (ticker plus logo), a search button and the theme toggle; the business list scrolls beneath. Search opens a centered native modal dialog (also ⌘K / Ctrl+K or `/`) that takes a ticker and lists companies recently opened in this browser (only ones the API answered for). Below 960px the rail dissolves into a top bar and sticky business chips.
- The company mark shows the ticker and its logo from the same public image host the main app uses (`images.financialmodelingprep.com`, the only external origin in CSP `img-src`, no referrer); a ticker monogram remains when it fails.
- Only API: `GET /api/business/v1/companies/:ticker`, plus the supplementary `/capital`, `/findings`, `/fundamentals`, `/events`, `/filings` and `/filings/:accession` under it. Reads go to the analysis pipeline (`spontra-analysis`) over the `EARNING_REPORT_PIPELINE` Service Binding to its `MapReads` named entrypoint (`workers/pipeline/src/index.ts`): a named entrypoint is unreachable from the public internet, so the binding is the credential and no key exists for this consumer; the pipeline serves it as the fixed identity `business-map` with the read scopes and its usual per-identity rate limit. Nothing from the browser's request is forwarded. Without the binding the data routes answer 503 and the shell still serves. No user-supplied URL; the only query accepted is the report pin on `/filings/:accession`. The SEC flow is schema-validated and unknown fields are stripped at every level. No company analysis envelope, private research, run state, portfolio, IBKR or generation routes are returned.
- Business dossier: each business (top level and child, e.g. 软件 / 软件许可) is explained by the pipeline's `BusinessExplainerWorkflow` — Tavily web search plus the current SEC filing and latest 10-K, after which the model reads that evidence and plans up to three follow-up searches on what is still missing about how the business operates (the harness caps, anchors and runs them). DeepSeek then writes each business from the angles its business model calls for — not a fixed template: up to six sections, each with a kind (delivery, customers, monetization, lifecycle, channel, economics, relation, other), a short title of its own and a layout (`steps` for an ordered chain such as license → deploy → renew, `list` for named entries, `prose`). An independent review removes single section items, products or fields, including a summary on its own (the card then shows the filing's description), and a business is dropped only when nothing verified is left; the shared harness (`harnessSections`) enforces each kind once, short unique titles, a layout its items can fill, cited items without amounts or percentages, and the caps, both when written and when the site reads it. Explanations stored before sections are read as the former four tabs. Every statement carries numbered links to the pages it was written from; unsupported fields, uncited claims and product names absent from the material are removed. No amounts. The site worker fetches `/api/analysis/v1/companies/:ticker/business-explainer` alongside the flow, re-validates it and returns it as `explainer`; when absent or invalid the curated descriptions remain. The Cron sweep starts at most one run per tick, only for AI-enabled tickers whose disclosed business list has changed, and retries a failed run weekly. Company analysis report paragraphs remain unexposed.
- Findings: what deserves attention in the report, listed in the rail under the business drawer (`FindingsList`), most severe first; 全部业务 is the drawer's header and its chevron opens the business rows, which stay open while a business is picked. A finding (`shared/analysis-contract/findings.ts`) names businesses, statement lines and a statement view, references the figures it rests on (flow metrics, business nodes, capital lines, SEC fundamentals, guidance items, each at a quarter or a four-quarter span, optionally compared year over year, quarter over quarter or against a guided range) and the chart that shows it; it carries no amounts. The page resolves every reference against the data it already draws (`shared/analysis-runtime/findings.ts`) and withholds a finding whose evidence does not resolve, whose anchors are absent from the quarter, or whose title or judgment states a number the evidence does not produce within the rounding written or two percent. In the overview each anchored Sankey node carries a numbered mark in the finding's colour; choosing a chip (or a mark) brings the stage to the finding's report and view, lights its nodes or pool items while the rest recedes, and replaces the trend with the lens: grouped bars or stacked shares bound to the same references, the cited judgment, each resolved figure with its comparison or guidance verdict, and what to watch next. 逐条看 walks the findings with the arrow keys; 对照 opens the paired finding (growth against its cost) beside it, each with its own chart and figures, and 聚焦此项 swaps them; Escape closes. A reference may also be a ratio of two figures at the same span (cloud revenue per dollar of capex), or remaining performance obligations: the `capital` quarters carry each filing's RPO total and its expected-timing rungs (typed start-date contexts in the revenue note, read from the archived inline-XBRL inventory; the SEC `fundamentals` series carries the total from companyfacts), and the `ladder` lens draws the balance over quarters with the rungs of the latest conversion schedule. Nothing is derived from the schedule: a filing that tags shares shows shares. Each finding's watch names the figure to look at next quarter or next fiscal year, optionally against a guidance item; once that period is published the lens shows the outcome (the figure, its comparison, and whether it landed inside guidance) and the chip carries a ↻ mark, otherwise it says which report it waits for. Selection is kept in `?finding=`. The site worker reads `/api/analysis/v1/companies/:ticker/findings` and the SEC `fundamentals` series through its own `/findings` and `/fundamentals` routes, re-validates both and strips provider and refresh state. The pipeline's findings workflow (`workers/pipeline/src/findings/workflow.ts`, started by the ten-minute sweep for AI-enabled companies when `FINDINGS_ENABLED` is true, at most one run per tick, once per report/guidance/narrative fingerprint) hands the model a ledger of already-resolved figures (`ledger.ts`) plus the company narrative as background, reads its JSON through the contract, verifies it with the same `verifyFindings` the page uses, repairs what was withheld in one further call, and publishes to `findings:v1:<ticker>` only what survives; nothing verifiable publishes nothing. Until a run has published for a company, a hand-written set serves where one exists (`workers/pipeline/src/findings/authored/ORCL.ts`).
- Narrative: each business carries the company's story as well as its figures (`shared/analysis-contract/business-narrative.ts`, read through `shared/analysis-runtime/business-narrative.ts`). A business the statements split out keeps its flow node id; one they do not (CoreWeave's three service layers inside a single reportable segment) joins the list under the flow node its revenue sits inside, as a qualitative row that lights that node, with no amount or share, and may name a rail anchor (remaining performance obligations) that the page resolves instead. Every row shows the business's stage (概念 / 示范 / 规模化 / 成熟 / 收缩, a fixed vocabulary) and, as dots, the status of each link of its thesis (已证实 / 进行中 / 未知 / 失效). Picking a business opens its dossier in the fixed reading order 能做什么 → 进度 (a dated timeline: done, planned, delayed with the original date) → 金主与客户 (grouped by role) → 对照 (the same customer need met different ways, this business and each alternative graded on the same dimensions; a cell opens the material behind it, and a cell nothing compares says 未比较) → 财报体现 (statement figures resolved on the page beside what they mean for this business) → 验证点 (the thesis chain: premise, status, evidence, failure condition, and the checks bound to figures); the explainer's own angles follow as further tabs. A layer the material does not cover says so rather than disappearing. While no business is picked the same card holds the company dossier: positioning, verdict, the company-level chain, industry position, checks, and each business with its stage. Prose bound to a figure (verdicts, premises, failure conditions, check conditions, tie meanings) may not restate one; milestones, parties and evidence quote contract terms from filings. The site worker reads `/api/v1/companies/:ticker/business-narrative` through its own `/narrative` route and re-validates it; until a workflow publishes for a ticker, a hand-written set serves where one exists (`workers/pipeline/src/narrative/authored/{CRWV,ORCL}.json`: CoreWeave shows the qualitative form under a single reportable segment, Oracle the form hung on quantified segments and their revenue nodes), and `vite dev` serves those authored files itself so the map can be previewed before the pipeline does.
- SEC fundamentals can supply partial same-company flow when a published full flow is absent. Unknown values stay missing. Financial/insurance, losses, negative expense reversals and incomplete statements use the existing disclosed-detail fallback.
- Uses `global_fetch_strictly_public` so any stray global fetch follows Cloudflare public routing; the pipeline is reached only through its binding. No database or scheduled task. Rate-limit binding: 60 requests/minute per IP. Missing limiter fails closed. Successful public output is cached for 60 seconds. SEC refresh remains in the existing backend, not page visits.
- Two small shared dependencies required by this public migration: safe fallback SEC accession lookup (CIK cannot be inferred from accession), and reject negative individual expense reversals before Sankey grouping. Other pending project-audit changes are excluded.

## Release

Push verified code to `origin/main`, following AGENTS.md. The pipeline build must have published `MapReads` before the map's build binds to it; both ride the same push, and the pipeline target deploys first in practice, but a map deploy that lands before the entrypoint exists fails its binding check and is simply redeployed by the next push. Existing Cloudflare Git CI builds/deploys the new app as a separate `spontra-business-map` Worker after the original application. Never deploy locally. CI name overrides are removed for the new Worker so it cannot replace the Spontra Worker. Assets and configuration are independent, while the release trigger currently shares the established main CI. A dedicated Git build target can be configured later without changing frontend/backend boundaries.

Initial complete-flow coverage: ORCL, actual public SEC quarters. Other companies depend on current backend availability; this does not assert universal extraction coverage. No fixture is imported by production code.
